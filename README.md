# Conducting-internal-security-audit
This repository is an activity in within google cybersecurity course in which i completed an internal securtiy audit
The project conducts a security audit for the company Botium Toys. The business has a single location as it started as a small company but Botium Toy’s online presence has grown, attracting customers in the U.S. and abroad.
As a result, their information technology (IT) department is under increasing pressure to support their online market worldwide. 
The IT manager has started by implementing the National Institute of Standards and Technology Cybersecurity Framework (NIST CSF), establishing an audit scope and goals, listing assets currently managed by the IT department, and completing a risk assessment. The goal of the audit is to provide an overview of the risks and/or fines that the company might experience due to the current state of their security posture.
My task is to review the managers scope, goals and risk assessment report, then, perform an internal audit by completing a controls and compliance checklist.
